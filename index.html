Hermes Studio

Hermes Studio is a private production tool operated by Saurabh Bhondekar. It writes, animates and renders original educational story videos, then stores the finished files in the operator's own Google Drive and, after manual review, publishes them to the operator's own YouTube channels.

It is not a public service: only the operator signs in to it, and it only accesses the operator's own Google account.
