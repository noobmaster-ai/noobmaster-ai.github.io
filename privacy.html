Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Hermes Studio ("the app") is a private video-production tool operated by Saurabh Bhondekar for his own Google account and YouTube channels.

What Google data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): the app creates folders and uploads video files, thumbnails and text notes it produced. With this scope it can only see files it created itself; it cannot read any other Drive content.
  • YouTube Data API (if enabled): the app uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads basic channel and video statistics for the operator's own channels.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload, organise and review the operator's own videos. OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted environment variables on the operator's private server. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Hermes Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Files stay in the operator's Drive and YouTube account until the operator deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer access the account.
